= My AI journey

[quote,Ethan Mollick,Co-Intelligence]
----
Users who intimately understand the nuances, limitations, and abilities of AI tools are uniquely positioned to unlock AI's full innovative potential. [...] Workers who figure out how to make AI useful for their jobs will have a large impact.
----

AI works best as a *thinking companion*, critiquing and refining our own choices and our own work, rather than fully delegating responsibility for decisions and outputs to AI…

AI *augments* human decision-making dskills - a useful "alien" mind that does not share the same biases and prejudices as we do.

We're still in an era of experimentation and evolution. Harness engineering and loop engineering, for example, are still in their infancy - and this is critical infrastructue to get right to run models reliably and securely…

Currently, there's no "user manual" for LLMs. Best practices are still emerging. And for each breakthrough model we have to learn over again what AI's new capabilities can do for us.
